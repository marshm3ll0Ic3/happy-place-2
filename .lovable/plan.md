# DiSTRUB: talent-first platform

## What changes

Make discovering and supporting overlooked talent the main experience, while preserving the dark purple identity, existing accounts, and useful pages. Deliver the brief in its four prescribed phases, completing and checking each before moving on.

## Phase 1 — Establish the product

- Replace the attention-exchange homepage with the 365-day talent-support model, clear demo status, talent discovery, applicant entry and a brand entry.
- Add a searchable talent catalogue and coherent fictional profiles in one initial niche. Profiles include skill evidence, selection summaries, starting metrics, programme dates, itemised budgets, public progress and precise outcome rules.
- Keep funding percentage, professional assessment and forecast probability visibly distinct. Show real platform activity as zero where no real records support it; keep illustrative activity explicitly separate.
- Update desktop, phone and tablet navigation. Move Value, Deal Room and School into secondary navigation; preserve their URLs and data. Point public-progress links to actual progress.
- Correct portfolio and order calculations with tests using the brief's concrete examples.
- Separate support money, commercial prices and forecast credits. **The brief replaces the old mock package top-up:** forecast credits become free and cannot be purchased, transferred, withdrawn or used for services. Preserve old balances and history without converting them into money or silently reusing them for the new simulation.
- Explain selection, privacy, support and forecast rules in linked How it works and Trust pages. Any unfinished action is explicitly unavailable, not a fake success or generic early-access primary button.

**Phase 1 check:** A visitor can search, open a matching profile, inspect its budget, progress and outcome rules, then return with filters preserved. Financial example tests pass; sample records are labelled throughout.

## Phase 2 — Programme operations

- Build saved, resumable applications, separate publication/commercial consents, private verification references and adult eligibility checks.
- Add authorised review, evidence, information requests, acceptance/decline reasons and appeal records. Acceptance does not automatically start a programme.
- Implement approved budgets, demo funding records, expense requests, operator approval, simulated vendor-payment records and public redacted progress.
- Use one UTC programme start and an end exactly 365 elapsed days later. Start only when prerequisites are satisfied; market prices never automatically stop essential support.
- Add role-specific applicant/talent and operator screens, with server-enforced ownership and access restrictions.

**Phase 2 check:** A fictional applicant can resume, submit, be reviewed and accepted, secure a demo budget, start, publish progress, process a simulated expense and reach final assessment without moving real money.

## Phase 3 — Forecasts and commercial journeys

- Implement saved YES/NO simulation positions, bounded disclosed liquidity, buys, partial sells, cancellations, fees, settlement and disputes. Reject insufficient funds, stale quotes and unsupported sells; make settlement idempotent.
- Split Portfolio into Support, Forecasts and Commercial orders with distinct balances, cost basis, realised results and unrealised estimates.
- Link service offers to talents, use named commercial currencies, and save requests, scope acceptance, delivery evidence and disputes. Unconfigured payments remain unavailable.
- Build research briefs, respondent consent, aggregated reports and permitted exports; keep private responses restricted.
- Add Insights with dated sources, baselines, sample sizes and clear separation of observations and forecasts.

**Phase 3 check:** Exercise partial fills/sells, no-liquidity rejection, repeat settlement, booking delivery and survey completion through saved records; verify another account cannot read private information.

## Phase 4 — Operational pilot

- Prepare configuration and handoff for jurisdictions, eligibility, payment corridor, providers, agreements, fees, refund/underfunding policies, resolver governance and commercial rights.
- Real payments, withdrawals, revenue/equity rights and real-money outcome trading stay blocked until the required arrangements are supplied and approved. These are external launch dependencies, not claims this build can resolve.

## Language and usability

- Complete English and French for new journeys, validation and states; preserve existing Spanish and Russian support.
- Verify layouts at 360, 390, 768 and 1440 pixels, plus increased zoom and keyboard use. Keep evidence and budgets before action panels on phones.
- Show loading, empty, failure and success states; preserve drafts and provide retries where relevant.

## Technical details

- Keep TanStack Start, existing Lovable Cloud authentication and existing account records; preserve immediate signup without email confirmation.
- Inspect existing schema and integrations before reuse. Apply additive database changes only; isolate demo records from existing financial history and operational totals.
- Use protected server functions for writes, server-enforced feature gates and explicit public-field allowlists. Keep identity/vendor files private and redact public evidence.
- Reuse semantic design tokens and existing controls. Add focused accounting, lifecycle, permissions and retry/idempotency tests alongside existing tests.
- Record architecture rules and maintain the phase checklist. At handoff, provide implemented routes, database changes, configured versus simulated integrations, actual acceptance results and named launch blockers.

## Delivery scope

Start with Phase 1, then proceed in order. Do not present the whole brief as finished when only discovery is built. Later operations depend on the earlier saved records; real financial launch remains gated on your approved operating arrangements.
